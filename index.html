Diagnostic & Hardware Recovery Guidance

  • 1
    Verify Port: Confirm ESP32 is recognized in Device Manager under Ports (COM & LPT).
  • 2
    Start AI Bridge: Double-click start_ai_bridge.bat in project root to launch WebSocket bridge.
  • 3
    Auto-Calibrate: Keep hand flat for 2 seconds to establish baseline ADC offsets before flexing.
  • 4
    Baud Check: Ensure firmware serial speed matches 115200 baud with newline termination.
